Surveillance Anomaly Detection

Mar 2026 - May 2026 | Weakly Supervised Video Anomaly Detection

📄 Paper Under Review (CESS 2026)

Project Overview

An end-to-end weakly supervised anomaly detection pipeline built on the UCF-Crime dataset, comprising 1,900 untrimmed surveillance videos. The system was trained and evaluated using only video-level labels, leveraging a pretrained 3D two-stream CNN as its backbone to learn spatio-temporal representations of normal and anomalous behavior.

Key Features

🎥 Weakly Supervised Learning

Trained and evaluated the pipeline using only video-level labels, avoiding the need for costly frame-level annotations.

🧩 Custom Image Processing Pipeline

Built entirely from scratch without high-level libraries — including frame sampling, resizing, filtering, and background subtraction — to enable efficient spatio-temporal feature extraction.

🎯 Foreground-Focused Representations

Engineered motion-based masking and background modelling to reduce environmental noise and improve anomaly separability.

📉 Improved Model Stability

The proposed image processing pipeline produced a more stable model that avoided overfitting, in contrast to a baseline relying only on the pretrained 3D two-stream CNN with a 3D ResNet backbone and minimal image processing.

Pipeline Architecture

✂️

Temporal Subsampling

01

This step involves selecting a subset of frames from each video to reduce redundancy. The video was downsampled from 30 fps to 5 fps using a fixed sampling ratio.

👾

Bilinear Interpolation

02

Applied bilinear interpolation to resize frames while maintaining spatial relationships, ensuring consistent input dimensions of 244 x 244 pixels for the subsequent processing steps.

👁️

Grayscale Conversion

03

Converted frames to grayscale to reduce computational complexity and focus on intensity variations for anomaly detection.

📊

Histogram Equalization

04

Enhanced image contrast through histogram equalization, improving the visibility of anomalies in the processed frames.

📎

Gaussian Smoothing

05

Applied Gaussian smoothing to reduce noise and enhance the overall quality of the processed frames.

🕳️

Background Subtraction

06

Implemented background subtraction to isolate moving objects and identify potential anomalies in the video sequence.

🤖

Morphological Operations

07

Applied morphological operations to refine the foreground masks and remove noise, improving the accuracy of anomaly detection.
